Write in Burmese using Unicode (NOT Zawgyi).
Sound like a native speaker, not a translation from English.
Never mix casual spoken and formal written registers in the same piece.
Register by tone:
- Friendly, Casual, Funny: casual spoken Burmese (ပါတယ်/တယ်/နော်)
- Professional, Formal: polite written Burmese (ပါသည်/သည်)
- If the tone is unclear, use polite spoken Burmese.
Keep brand names and common tech terms in English.
Hashtags may mix Burmese and English.
Avoid awkward literal translations and overly bookish words in casual content.
